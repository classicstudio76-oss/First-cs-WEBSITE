About Classic Studio

Located in the heart of Kamalnagar, Chitwan, Bharatpur, Classic Studio has been providing exceptional photography and videography services for over a decade. Our passion is to capture life's most precious moments with creativity, technical expertise, and an artistic eye.

We specialize in wedding photography, event coverage, product shoots, portraits, and commercial videography. Our team of experienced professionals uses state-of-the-art equipment to deliver stunning visuals that tell your unique story.

At Classic Studio, we believe every moment is worth preserving. Whether it's the joy of a wedding, the energy of a corporate event, or the elegance of a product, we ensure each image and video reflects the essence of the occasion.
